Academic support

You are not on your own

Every department is headed by an academic who is accountable for the programmes it teaches. Coursework is marked against published criteria, and the portal keeps your submissions, feedback and results on the record.

If you are stuck with coursework, a result you do not understand, or something that is affecting your studies, speak to your department first — or raise a support ticket and let it come to them.

  • {% include "portal/partials/icon.html" with name="chat" size=16 %} Coursework or a result — start with your lecturer, then your head of department.
