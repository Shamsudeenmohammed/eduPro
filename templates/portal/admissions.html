The process

Four steps from application to enrolment

The same four steps apply to every application type, whether you are applying as a new student or returning for another qualification.

  1. Choose your programme

    Pick a programme from the list and review its entry requirements, duration and the documents the admissions office asks for.

  2. Complete the online form

    Enter your personal and contact details, select your programme and application type, and save a draft if you need more time to gather documents.

  3. Submit and track

    Submit before the intake closes, then follow every stage — screening, review, payment and decision — with your reference number.

  4. Accept and enrol

    Accept your offer, complete enrolment and sign in to the portal for registration, fees, course materials and results.
